# Clarivo product scope

## Product statement

Clarivo helps users articulate their thoughts clearly, concisely, and
coherently in spoken Vietnamese through practice, concrete analysis, targeted
feedback, and retry.

Clarivo V1 is not a pronunciation trainer.

## Initial audience

Vietnamese-speaking students and young professionals preparing for interviews,
presentations, project explanations, academic discussion, and workplace
communication.

## Core problems

- The main point appears too late.
- The answer lacks a clear structure.
- Ideas or phrases are repeated.
- Fillers and false starts reduce fluency.
- The answer is long but carries little information.
- Examples are missing or too vague.
- The speaker loses their train of thought or drifts off topic.

## Core loop

```text
Choose practice
→ Receive prompt
→ Prepare
→ Speak
→ Review transcript and analysis
→ Apply the top improvements
→ Retry
→ Compare attempts
```

Features that do not support this loop are outside the initial scope.

## V1 exercise types

- `DAILY_60`
- `OPINION`
- `INTERVIEW`
- `TECHNICAL_EXPLANATION`
- `STORYTELLING`
- `SUMMARY`

## V1 scoring dimensions

Delivery:

- Fluency
- Pace

Content:

- Relevance
- Structure
- Conciseness
- Coherence

Pronunciation, confidence, and multilingual modes are future modules.
